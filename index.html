FAQ

Questions people ask before they start

Is TourneysHQ free to use?

Yes. The Free plan lets you run tournaments with up to 8 teams. Paid plans add more teams and extra tools.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can start straight away. Your tournaments are saved in your browser on the device you use.

Which tournament formats can I use?

Round-robin, knockout, and groups followed by knockout.

Can I use it for any sport or game?

Yes. Football, basketball, volleyball, esports, board games and more all work, because you decide who plays whom.

Can I print or share the results?

Yes. Club and League plans include printable reports and share-ready graphics.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Tournaments saved in your browser would be removed, so keep a printed or shared copy of anything important.
